Privacy Policy for Better Loan Calc

Effective date: November 7, 2026

Better Loan Calc is an iOS application published by Daniel Tartakovsky. This page describes the app's privacy practices and provides information about features that may be included in future versions.

Information you enter

Better Loan Calc allows you to enter loan calculation inputs such as amounts, interest rates, and terms. Depending on the features available in your installed version, you may also choose to enter or save project-related information. Avoid entering sensitive information that is not necessary for your use of the app.

Storage and data handling

Information entered into the app may be stored on your device to support its features. Removing the app may delete locally stored data. The exact storage and synchronization behavior depends on the version and features you use.

Advertising and third-party services

Some versions of Better Loan Calc may include advertising or consent-management technology from Google, such as Google AdMob. If advertising is enabled in your installed version, Google and its partners may process device identifiers, app interactions, approximate location inferred from network information, and other information as described in Google's policies and subject to applicable consent choices. Advertising is not necessarily active in every version of the app.

Learn more about Google's Privacy Policy and Google's advertising privacy guidance.

Permissions

Any access to device features, such as photos, is subject to the permissions and controls presented by iOS and the features you choose to use.

Your choices

You can review or change applicable app permissions in iOS Settings. If advertising consent choices are offered in your version of the app, use those controls to manage your preferences. You can remove locally stored app information using available app controls or by deleting the app, noting that deletion may be irreversible.

Children

Better Loan Calc is a general-purpose calculation and productivity app and is not specifically directed to children.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when app features or data practices change. The current version will be posted at this URL.

Questions

For app support and privacy-related inquiries, please see the support page.

Version note: Features and third-party integrations may vary by app release. This policy should be reviewed against the actual production app's data collection, SDK configuration, and App Store privacy disclosures before enabling new advertising or synchronization features.
