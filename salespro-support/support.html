Better Loan Calc Support

Help and information for the Better Loan Calc iOS app.

Getting started

Enter a loan amount, interest rate, and loan term to calculate estimated payments. Results are estimates; actual lender terms may differ.

Troubleshooting

If the app does not respond as expected, close and reopen it and make sure you are using the latest available version. Before making changes to your device, preserve any important app data. Do not delete the app as a troubleshooting step, since doing so may remove locally stored information.

Need more help?

Visit the App Store listing and use the available developer contact or support options. Please do not send passwords, banking credentials, or sensitive personal data in a support request.
